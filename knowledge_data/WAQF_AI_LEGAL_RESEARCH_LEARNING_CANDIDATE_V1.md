## Verified lessons

1. **Raw skill prompt text is not a safe admission mechanism.**
   A 3B model may increase recall while also increasing false positives. External methodology must be converted into structured workflow and deterministic gates where possible.

2. **Article number is not legal identity.**
   Extraction and audit must bind: legal instrument → article/provision → exact operative text. The same article number can exist in unrelated statutes.

3. **Retrieved source is not automatically required evidence.**
   Required citations must be selected semantically from the question and verified claims. Forcing every retrieved source into the answer creates noise and false failures.

4. **Court-role separation is a hard legal gate.**
   Party grounds, lower-court quotations, court reasoning, holding, and disposition must remain distinct. Court-body detection must be adapted to real judgment structure.

5. **Citation syntax validity is insufficient.**
   The proposition must belong to the cited source. Citation-number range checks alone cannot detect source ownership errors.

6. **Deterministic-first legal synthesis reduces hallucination.**
   Direct statutory and historical propositions should be rendered from verified claims. LLM synthesis should be reserved for bounded inference/qualification.

7. **Qualifiers must survive compression.**
   Evidence compression may not drop conditions such as retention of raqaba by Bayt al-Mal or jurisdictional limitations.

8. **Historical creation evidence is not later legal classification.**
   A 1552 waqfiyya can prove historical endowment evidence without proving a later Ottoman Land Code classification for every parcel.

9. **Registry names are not self-proving classifications.**
   A record labeled Haseki Sultan waqf does not by itself prove waqf takhsisat.

10. **Hardware changes invalidate model capability measurements.**
    Adding GTX 1050 required new hardware fingerprinting and rerunning model benchmarks; old CPU-only results became stale.

11. **Small local models can be useful under controlled decomposition.**
    Qwen/Llama 3B are not trusted as unconstrained legal judges, but can perform bounded synthesis when deterministic evidence and audits constrain them.

12. **External skills must be pinned and adapted.**
    Adopt → inspect → license/security review → pin exact commit/hash → adapt → A/B → regression → Mind review → Workspace decision.

13. **Learning and promotion are separate.**
    Experience capture and learning-candidate generation can be automatic; canonical skill/knowledge promotion must remain external to Waqf AI.
